# Review rules

One row per signal: what it reads, the minimum data before it speaks, the thresholds, and the messages. The code is `src/domain/review/signals.ts`; the trend maths is `src/domain/calc/trend.ts`. PLAN.md section 6 wins on any conflict. Every number in the messages below is a placeholder filled from the records; the sentence shape is what the tests pin.

## Assembly

- Signals are listed in priority order: protein, sessions, weight trend, lifts, sets per body part, cardio.
- At most three positives and three attention items, taken in that order. Neutral rows stay in `signals` (and `neutral`); to-unlock rows sit under `to_unlock` with the exact count still needed and never under attention.
- The focus is the top attention item's action, or "Keep doing exactly this." when there is none.
- `basis` reads "3 sessions, 2 check-ins, 5 days of food."
- `inputs_hash` is a stable hash of every record passed in except the bundled library and the derived trend, so an edited past set changes it and the stored review is recomputed.
- The badge on attention items reads "Needs attention"; the UI never says "negative" or "failed".
- The disclaimer is one constant: general fitness information, not medical advice; see a professional if pregnant, under 18, on medication that affects weight, or with a history of disordered eating.

## Guardrails (same module, tested)

- Calorie floor: the larger of BMR and 1200 kcal (female), 1500 kcal (male), 1350 kcal (unspecified).
- The deficit never exceeds 25 percent; the rate target never exceeds 1 percent of body weight per week.
- Loss faster than 1.5 percent per week for two check-ins reads slow down whatever the goal.
- Logged intake under the floor on 3 or more days gets a logging check, never praise.
- Banned phrases: burns fat, boosts metabolism, detox, cures, and any condition name (`CONDITION_NAMES`).
- No exclamation marks on attention items; every sentence names a number; every message ends in one action.
- The 7700 kcal per kg estimate holds for short windows only.

## Trend maths (`src/domain/calc/trend.ts`)

- One point per reading; duplicates by date collapse to the latest; extra weigh-ins between check-ins count.
- Theil-Sen slope on elapsed days over the last 28 days, in percent of the latest trend weight per week.
- The 95 percent interval on the slope comes from the fit's own residuals (Student t on n minus 2 degrees of freedom), so a noisy series never earns a band verdict.
- EWMA line with an 8-day half-life on irregular spacing.
- Precision: early at 4 readings over 12 days, full at 7 over 24; the "n of 10" line counts readings in the window against the 10 expected at a strict 4-day cadence.
- Direction: down at or under minus 0.1 percent per week, up at or over plus 0.1, flat between.
- Worked examples pinned by tests: weekly points 80.0, 79.7, 79.3, 79.0 give about minus 0.34 kg per week and read on track; 82.0, 81.8, 81.6, 81.5 on days 0, 4, 8, 12 give a directional read only; adding 81.2, 81.1, 80.9 on days 16, 20, 24 earns the on-track verdict; the same spacing with 1 kg of scatter stays "not yet precise".
